How a session works

You can contact me by email or telephone and we will agree on a time to meet on Team, Google Meet or FaceTime.

A first session lasts between an hour and an hour and a half.

This first meeting allows me to begin to know you and understand what led you to come to see me.

Together we discuss possible options and you choose, at the end of our discussion, whether you wish to continue and make a new appointment or take the time to think after this first discussion.

Then, as we start working together, each session usually lasts an hour. However, sometimes we will take some more time if it is necessary and as it is possible.

Session fees

Each session costs 60 euros but I adapt my fee to students, people looking for work or in financial difficulties.
